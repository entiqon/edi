# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and
harassment‑free experience for everyone contributing to Entiqon EDI.

We pledge to make participation respectful and professional regardless
of: - Experience level - Gender identity or expression - Sexual
orientation - Disability - Ethnicity - Religion - Nationality

## Expected Behavior

-   Be respectful and constructive.
-   Accept feedback professionally.
-   Focus on technical merit and architectural integrity.
-   Maintain high documentation and testing standards.

## Unacceptable Behavior

-   Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
-   Personal attacks or inflammatory language.
-   Publishing private information without permission.
-   Repeatedly ignoring project architecture rules.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do
not align with this Code of Conduct.

Serious violations may result in temporary or permanent bans.

Keep it professional. Keep it constructive. Keep it clean.
